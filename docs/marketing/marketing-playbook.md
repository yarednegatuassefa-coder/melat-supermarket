# Melat Supermarket Marketing Playbook
Goal: grow nearby awareness, communicate genuine offers, and encourage repeat visits without inaccurate stock/price expectations.

## Audience / positioning
Nearby households and apartment/compound residents; budget-conscious shoppers who value convenience and clear ETB prices, with Amharic/English copy where useful. Working position: convenient neighborhood supermarket for everyday shopping with clear pricing and dependable service. Validate; no unsupported lowest-price/widest-selection claims.

## Message pillars / channels
Convenience and location; current ETB prices; useful assortment; local trust. Use accurate verified business profile, real photos, owner-approved offer cards, category highlights, and simple in-store signage. Messaging must be consent-based.

## Campaign workflow
Choose category/goal → owner verifies stock, shelf/POS price, margin, terms and dates → draft bilingual copy and one CTA → check spelling/price/availability → owner approves → publish manually → review aggregate results.

## Measurement / boundaries
Aggregate visits, offer redemption, category sales, gross margin, repeat-purchase indicators. No individual purchase history in marketing. Never write POS, inventory, accounting, tax, supplier, cash, or payment records. No fake reviews, unapproved discounts, stale stock claims, ads, or publication without approval.
